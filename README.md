# Mitchell & Ness (mitchell-and-ness)
Vintage and throwback sports apparel brand founded in Philadelphia in 1904. Holds league-licensed agreements with the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, and MLS to produce authentic reproduction jerseys, snapback hats, varsity jackets, hoodies, and shorts. Acquired in February 2022 by Fanatics, Inc. alongside investors Jay-Z (Marcy Venture Partners), LeBron James / Maverick Carter (SpringHill Co), and Kevin Hart.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Authentic Throwback Jerseys | Reproduction wool-flannel and mesh jerseys built from the company's archive of historical league uniforms. |
| Hardwood Classics Collection | NBA-licensed throwback line introduced in 1999 that drove revenue from $1.5M (1998) to $23M (2002). |
| Snapback Headwear | Vintage-style snapback caps across NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, and college team marks. |

| Artist & Cultural Collaborations | Collaborative drops with musicians, artists, and cultural figures. |
